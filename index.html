Keyboard: Enter start · V vaccinated · O overload · arrows pick. PS5: press any button first, then ✕ start · □ vaccinated · △ overload · d-pad picks.

How to play

You are a macrophage on a glass slide. Fire antibodies at the virions; each hit splits a capsid into smaller particles until nothing is left. Variants glow red and take two hits. Catch IFN for a shield, mAb for a three-way spray, mRNA for homing antibodies, CD4 for helper T cells, FLG for flagella (faster, tighter steering) and +1 for an extra cell (mitosis).

New viruses arrive every passage, and so do new weapons: the complement cascade (passage 3), the perforin lance on X / □ (5), phage therapy drones (8) and the neutrophil NET (11).

The mRNA vaccine start gives you homing antibodies for the first 12 seconds of every passage. Viral Overload never ends: keep the viral load under the red line, and spend a cell on a cytokine storm (C / △) when it gets bad.
